Extract tables from PDFs, with provenance.

Five independent PDF engines extract the table. A cell's value is kept when at least 3 of the 5 agree on it (3 of 4 where pdftotext cannot run); otherwise the cell is left blank, with each engine's reading.

Your PDF is not uploaded. It is read in this browser tab and stays on your computer.
